Back in May, I spent a couple of days fly fishing for Northern Pike with my friend and owner of PikexFly in Western North Dakota. Bob is an excellent guide and I always have a blast fishing with him. The huge tug on the fly and the fight that ensues when a big Pike attacks is a thrill that cannot be understated. I haven't had the pleasure of catching a 40+ inch Pike, but Bob has sent me countless photos of him with those large beasts.

My arm got tired from the 12 hours of casting so I took a breather and some photos of Bob as he was fishing.

If you are ever interested in fly fishing for Northern Pike in Western North Dakota, reach out to Bob on his website [www.pikexfly.com](https://www.pikexfly.com).
